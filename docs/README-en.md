# Flamingo/Strat — quick guide

Community Rainbow Six Siege callout and tactics tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Ubisoft or R6Calls. Third-party names, artwork and badges retain their owners' rights.

Open https://guidufox.github.io/breach-r6-atlas/ or keep the portable app files together and open index.html. Choose English in the language menu. Use the sun/moon button to switch between light and dark mode; the choice is remembered. Each sidebar has one visible control to open or close it. Draw routes, place operator badges, assign five player tasks, practise callouts and export your plans.

Team rooms sync through HTTPS, support five participants and last up to two hours. Personal work stays separate; download the full map strategy before leaving. Invitations allow editing and should stay within the team.

The canonical documentation, rights, privacy and maintenance instructions are linked in [README.md](../README.md). Public contact: https://github.com/Guidufox/breach-r6-atlas/issues/new.
