## Mensaje preparado

Hello R6Calls maintainers,

I maintain Flamingo/Strat, a community callout and tactics tool initially built for a group of five friends learning Rainbow Six Siege. Its current public URL is https://guidufox.github.io/breach-r6-atlas/ and the source repository is https://github.com/Guidufox/breach-r6-atlas.

The app uses R6Calls map SVGs and map-specific callout dictionaries. Callout tables may also appear on static reference pages, and the portable version includes offline copies.

The project has no advertising, subscriptions or charges. It includes an explicit notice that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ubisoft or R6Calls, a public removal contact, and a procedure to stop distributing disputed assets.

Could you confirm in writing whether you permit this distribution of the maps and callout dictionaries you can authorize, and whether you require particular attribution or other conditions? If you are not the right contact, please point us to the appropriate maintainer.

This request does not presume that R6Calls can license Ubisoft's or any other third party's artwork or trademarks. We would review any additional conditions before accepting them and would seek separate authorization where needed.
